Connect to Jarvis on your PC

This Mac uses the Jarvis that runs on your PC, through Tailscale. Your conversations, memory, cameras and settings stay on the PC.

  1. Open Tailscale on this Mac and sign in with the same account as the PC.
  2. On your phone or PC, open Jarvis’s phone page and copy its address (the part ending in .ts.net).

Type the code

Jarvis just showed a six-digit code on your PC and sent it to your phone. Typing it here lets this Mac use the full Jarvis, settings included. You only do this once.
